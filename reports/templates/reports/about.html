About I-V Tree

Protecting trees through community action

I-V Tree is a community conservation project designed to help people report and monitor trees affected by invasive ivy.

Users can submit tree reports, share progress updates and monitor how reported trees change over time. By bringing these reports together, I-V Tree provides a simple way for communities to record local conservation activity.
